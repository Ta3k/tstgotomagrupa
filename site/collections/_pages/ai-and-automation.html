Where the conversation usually begins

Companies rarely come to us with a question about AI. They come with a problem: enquiries remain unanswered for too long, documents circulate by email, data has to be copied between systems, or some information is too sensitive to leave the company.

Only then does it become clear that one issue touches several areas at once – process, software, integrations and sometimes hardware and compliance. That raises the question: who should lead the project?

How we run an AI project

We do not begin by choosing a tool. We begin with the process, because only the process shows whether AI is the right answer and, if so, where it should be used.

1. Conversation

You describe the situation. We identify the problem and determine which Group companies are needed.

2. Analysis

We examine the process, data and systems. We identify the areas with the greatest potential and those where AI would add no value.

3. Pilot

A limited scope with evaluation criteria agreed in advance. The decision on next steps comes after the pilot, not before it.

4. Implementation and development

Production deployment, maintenance and expansion into further areas.

People remain responsible for decisions

This is a principle we design around, not a feature that can be switched off.

AI-based mechanisms operate with high, but not absolute, certainty. In every implementation we therefore define in advance what the system can do independently and what requires employee approval. This particularly applies to external communications and matters involving professional responsibility, including accounting, tax, legal and commercial decisions.

In practice, the solution must also leave an audit trail: it should be clear what an answer was based on and who approved it.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to know which AI solution I need?

No. The conversation begins with the process and the problem, not with a technology choice. The solution is selected as a result of the analysis, not as a prerequisite.

Will AI replace employees in these processes?

The solutions we implement are designed to support the team. They prepare, organise and suggest, while decisions that carry responsibility remain with people.

Does company data have to be sent to external AI tools?

Not always. Processing can take place in an environment controlled by the company. GOTOMA GENERAL explains the factors behind this choice under Private AI and local environments.

Does working with several Group companies mean several contracts and several conversations?

We divide the scope internally. The billing model and form of cooperation are agreed individually for each project.

Where should I begin if I only have a general sense that something could be improved?

Describe one process that takes the most time. That is enough for an initial conversation.
